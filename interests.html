Welcome to my interests page! Here you can find out a little more about the things I enjoy doing and some of the activities that take up my spare time. Two of my main interests are watching Formula 1 (F1) and playing netball.
I enjoy watching the different F1 teams and drivers, keeping up with the races and seeing how each season unfolds. I also enjoy playing netball, as it keeps me active, allows me to challenge myself and gives me the chance to work as part of a team. These interests are a big part of what I enjoy doing outside of school, and I'm excited to share a little more about them!




-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One of my main interests is playing netball, which I really enjoy as it allows me to stay active, challenge myself and spend time with others. I play for Rolleston Netball Club, where I compete in games throughout the season. I enjoy being part of a team, working together with my teammates and supporting each other during games. Netball has also helped me improve my skills, build confidence and develop my teamwork. I look forward to each season and enjoy the different experiences and challenges that come with playing.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I also enjoy watching Formula 1 and keeping up with the excitement of each race weekend. My favourite drivers are Oscar Piastri and Liam Lawson, and I especially enjoy seeing how they perform throughout the season. I like the competitiveness of the sport and how unpredictable each race can be, as anything can happen right up until the final lap. I also enjoy following the different teams, learning about the drivers and seeing how they work together with their engineers to improve their cars. Each race weekend brings something different, whether it's an exciting overtake, a close battle between drivers or an unexpected result. I find Formula 1 really interesting because it combines speed, skill, teamwork and strategy, which makes every season enjoyable to follow.
